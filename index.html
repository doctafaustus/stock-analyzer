Volume Momentum

Rising on heavy trading: up 8%+ this month on 1.25× normal volume, above its 50-day average. Winners on heavy volume tend to keep running. Buy strength.

Pullback in Uptrend

Strong stock that just dipped: up 15%+ in 6 months, above its 200-day average, down 3–12% this week, RSI under 45. Dips in strong trends often bounce. Buy the dip, use a stop.

Range Bounce

Sideways stock swinging up and down in a steady 8–25% channel for ~6 weeks, now near the bottom with RSI under 40. Tends to swing back up. Target the middle or top of the range; stop just below the low.

How strong an example of its setup a stock is, ranked against every stock scanned (S&P 500 + Nasdaq-100). Volume Momentum blends its 1-month gain, relative volume and 6-month gain. Pullback in Uptrend blends its 6-month strength and the size of this week’s dip. Range Bounce blends how close it is to the range bottom, how often it has swung, and how oversold it is. 80+ is a textbook example; 50 is middle of the pack. It is not a probability of profit.

How stocks make this list

Healthy: profitable, positive free cash flow, return on equity 8%+, earnings not down more than 15%, debt under 2.5× equity. Liquid: $50M+ traded per day.
